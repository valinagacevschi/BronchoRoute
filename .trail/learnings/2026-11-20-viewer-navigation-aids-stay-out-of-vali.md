## Decided
New corner minimap, green distance rings and the end-of-route reveal are interactive-only. All aids draw behind an exportFrame.active guard so stills and recorded video keep their previous clean pixels.
Reveal runs only when the endoluminal frame is not posed: offline fly-through passes an explicit pose and keeps its existing end-hold.
Generated renderer.html is rebuilt by splicing the previously embedded payload into the new template; the template alone is not enough.

## Tried and ruled out
Rendering the reveal through the Anatomical variant: would change the user's variant and lose endoluminal state. Reused a fixed outside camera derived from the route tangent instead.
Client-side route planning for branch clicks: rejected earlier; branch double-click reuses the server route-target call.

## Watch out
Aids without the export guard changed export pixels and broke existing image checks.
Reveal without the posed guard altered recorded video frames.
Single click on a branch keeps its review meaning; retarget is double-click only, otherwise the remove-branch panel and retarget fight.
